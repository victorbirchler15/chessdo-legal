FAQ

How do I play online?

Play → Online, pick your time control (5 min, 10 min or 3 days per move): we find you an opponent at your level. To play a friend: Friends tab → Add a friend (share your link), then "Challenge".

How do I play without an opponent?

Play → Vs. bot, pick a level (easy, medium, hard) and start the game.

Where did my ongoing game go?

It's on the home screen: tap it to pick up where you left off.

How do I unlock heroes?

Earn badges (Profile tab) and reach point milestones by playing online: each one unlocks a new hero.

A player has an offensive username or behavior?

During the game, open the reactions (speech bubble) → "Report" or "Block". In 3-day games, tap their avatar. You can also email me at the address above.
