Chess Do

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Chess Do collects the bare minimum needed to run the online game. No password: you just pick a username the first time you open the app. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and only used to restore your progress on another device.

What the app collects

  • A username that you choose on first launch.
  • A player ID, randomly generated by the app (not linked to your name, phone number or contacts).
  • Your online games: moves played, result, league points.
  • If you allow them: a notification token, only used to tell you about challenges and your opponent's moves.
  • Your phone's language (French or English), so notifications reach you in your language.
  • If you report a player: the report (player concerned, reason), to moderate inappropriate usernames and behavior. The players you block, so you are no longer matched with them.
  • If you sign in with Apple (optional): the ID provided by Apple and, depending on your choice, your email address or a private relay address hidden by Apple.
  • If you sign in with Google (optional): the ID provided by Google and your email address.

Where this data is stored

It is hosted by Supabase, in the European Union. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites (no ATT prompt).

Local games and practice games against the bot never leave your device.

Usage analytics

To improve the game, we measure a few usage events: app opens, online games started and finished (time control, hero, result), quick match searches, challenges and reports. These are tied to your random player ID, never to your username or email, and hosted by PostHog in the European Union. They are only used for internal statistics, never for advertising.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report (screen, iPhone model, iOS and app version, error trace) is sent to Sentry, hosted in the European Union, so we can fix the problem. These reports contain neither your username, nor your email, nor your player ID.

Moderation

Usernames are visible to other players (online games, leaderboard). Offensive usernames are automatically rejected. During a game, you can report or block a player: every report is reviewed within 24 hours, and inappropriate usernames are renamed.

Deleting your data

Right from the app: Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete my account. Your player profile, points, unlocked heroes, friends list and blocks are erased immediately. In your opponents' game history, your username is replaced with "Deleted player".

You can also email victorbirchler15@gmail.com with your username.
